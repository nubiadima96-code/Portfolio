Mobile product case study

HouseHub
Realtor in Pocket

A unified mobile app for residents, landlords, tenants, and building management. It combines utility indicators, online payments, house communication, service requests, and local real estate in one flow.

Transfer of indicators Utility payments House chats and notices Real estate module

Main visual. Full user flow is shown below by screens 1 to 7.

Project context

The product addresses a common problem in apartment buildings: fragmented tools for everyday tasks. Residents usually switch between messenger groups, bank apps, and separate utility or management websites. HouseHub centralizes this into one mobile experience with clear roles and structured communication.

Core value: less chaos in communication, faster utility routines, transparent building processes, and direct access to housing actions (rent/buy/list) inside the same ecosystem.

Onboarding and entry

The first flow starts with registration or login. Users can sign in by phone and password, recover access quickly, verify by SMS code, and complete basic personal data. The goal is a fast start and immediate access to building services.

assets/HouseHub/2.png

Dashboard and monthly control

The dashboard combines monthly utility usage, cost visibility per unit, alerts, and building news in one place. Users can monitor multiple addresses/apartments and receive reminders for upcoming payments or urgent house events.

assets/HouseHub/3.png

Detailed analytics

Extended statistics help users switch between day, week, and month views for electricity, gas, and water. This supports spending awareness and helps identify peaks in consumption before they become expensive.

assets/HouseHub/4.png

Services, indicators, and payments

Users can create service requests (including lift or technical issues), submit indicators for water, electricity, gas, and waste, and pay either per service or all at once. Payment options include cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, keeping the whole utility flow in one app.

assets/HouseHub/5.png

Real estate module

A full real estate path is included: search form, result lists, advanced filtering, and detailed property pages. This allows users to rent, buy, or list apartments without jumping to external marketplaces.

assets/HouseHub/6.png

Structured communication

House chats are organized by context: HOA, entrance, building, service teams, and direct support. This reduces noise compared to generic messenger groups and keeps requests, updates, and replies visible and actionable.

assets/HouseHub/7.png

Settings and personalization

The settings area gives control over notifications, payment methods, language, and account preferences. A support/help center is part of the roadmap to complete the service lifecycle inside the product.

Why this approach

  • One app instead of fragmented tools (messengers + banks + websites)
  • Clear utility workflow: indicator transfer and payment in one path
  • Transparent communication with residents and building management
  • Real estate actions integrated into the same product ecosystem
  • Designed for frequent routines: daily communication and monthly utility tasks
