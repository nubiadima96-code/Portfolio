About the project

I participated in a large corporate project focused on the design and presentation of AI solutions across multiple products. The goal was to integrate advanced AI features to increase productivity, simplify workflows, and improve user experience.

My role included creating dynamic animations, designing interfaces, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to present complex AI capabilities in a visually engaging and understandable way for online meetings, email, CMS/CRM, and data workflows.

Role UX Designer · Motion / Animation
Focus AI scenarios, UX flows, UI elements
Context Online meetings, email, CMS/CRM, data

Process

  • Understanding AI features: collaboration with developers, product managers, and the UX team to deeply understand the purpose, mechanics, and value of each AI feature for users.
  • Animations and design: creating dynamic animations to clearly demonstrate AI functionality, with a focus on clarity and engagement.
  • UI elements: participation in designing clear interfaces for each feature: accessibility and alignment with the project vision.
  • Feedback and refinements: iterations on design and animation based on feedback so the outcome matches stakeholder expectations and project goals.

Result

The project clearly showed how AI can transform communication, data workflows, and product interaction. My animations and UI design helped present complex functionality in an attractive and understandable way for clients, the team, and presentations.
