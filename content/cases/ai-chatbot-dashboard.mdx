An AI chatbot that replaces the initial sales/briefing stage, plus a dashboard for managers to prepare them before client contact and improve lead quality.

The chatbot was designed to understand client needs through natural conversation, qualifying leads and collecting project requirements before any human interaction. The manager dashboard aggregates these conversations into actionable insights.
