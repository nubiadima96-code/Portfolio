A unified mobile app for residents, landlords, tenants, and building management. It combines utility indicators, online payments, house communication, service requests, and local real estate in one flow.

The challenge was to create a single product that serves multiple user roles—residents tracking utility consumption, landlords managing properties, tenants communicating with management, and building administrators overseeing everything. I designed role-based navigation and adaptive interfaces that show relevant features based on the user's role.
