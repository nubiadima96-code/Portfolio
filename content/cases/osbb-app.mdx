As part of a course, I created a mobile app for residential complex residents - a full product with thoughtful logic and UX.

The app covers utility bill payments, camera access, service requests, community announcements, and consumption analytics. I designed the entire user flow from onboarding to daily use, focusing on simplifying complex municipal processes into clear mobile interfaces.
