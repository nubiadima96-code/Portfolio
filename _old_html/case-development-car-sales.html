Overview

This is my first design project. Why did I decide to create a car buying and selling app? Before the coronavirus, I was buying a car and I was not comfortable with the search with a car and saw that most car applications are inconvenient and incomprehensible to newbies. This was the reason for the creation of CarsZona.

assets/Case 1/case1-search-visual.png

Search

On this screen, the user can find any car by brand, and by any parameters, the user can choose the year of the car, how many seats and the class of the car. And the location and indicate the price for which he wants to buy the car.
