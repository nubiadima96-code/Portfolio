Why Ukrainian
railway?

While my aged parents were trying to buy a ticket through the Ukrainian Railways application, since they did not trust third-party resources (they were afraid of fraud on their part) while using the application, they had many difficulties at every step, and they asked me for help in buying a ticket, and even I did not immediately understand how to buy that ticket. That’s why I set myself the task of an internal project, a redesign of the Ukrainian railway app.

Discovery

The discovery stage of the project began by exploring the functionality of the existing legacy system. Further discovery meetings were conducted to reveal business requirements about the new capabilities of the product. Based on these discoveries, the team defined the scope in the form of information architecture, clarified types of users with their accesses, and produced user flows for main scenarios.
